# Non-goals (v1)

We will **not**:
- Move money automatically, place trades, or submit loan applications. The user confirms every action.
- Name stocks, funds or specific policies as recommendations, or rank products by commission (Recommendation Firewall).
- Suggest new unsecured borrowing as the first fix.
- Let the LLM calculate or decide. Rules calculate; AI explains.
- Show any leaderboard across families, or rank anyone by money. Family leaderboard ranks habits only.
- Give points for taking loans or buying products; points have no cash value.
- Infer health, caste, religion or "worth" from any data.
- Use Aadhaar for account discovery, or ask for Aadhaar/PAN for planning.
- Keep raw AA payloads beyond 24 hours.
- Request investment FI types (equities, MF) in v1 — only DEPOSIT, RECURRING_DEPOSIT, INSURANCE_POLICIES.
- Build ML models in v1 — detection is rules-based.
- Build a native Android app (PWA only; Capacitor APK is optional stretch).
- Claim legal certification. We show design within known constraints.
